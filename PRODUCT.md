# TradeEngine

## Register

product

## Users

TradeEngine is operated by a technically proficient trader monitoring automated and paper-trading workflows from terminal and web dashboards. The primary job is to understand system state quickly, inspect signals and trades, follow active engine cycles, and intervene safely when needed.

## Product Purpose

TradeEngine combines market-data ingestion, strategy evaluation, model analysis, risk controls, execution, and operational monitoring. Its terminal dashboard is the compact control surface; its web app provides expanded analysis, signal, portfolio, and live-pipeline views. Success means the operator can distinguish asset classes, strategies, sessions, risk states, and execution outcomes without ambiguity, including while an engine cycle is actively updating the interface.

## Brand Personality

Precise, operational, and calm. Copy should be concise and technically accurate, with terminology that remains consistent across navigation, headings, empty states, and status messages.

## Anti-references

Avoid decorative trading-terminal theatrics, vague category labels, marketing language, and interfaces that depend on color alone. Do not obscure operational meaning behind novelty or visual effects.

## Design Principles

1. Use domain-accurate language at every level of the information hierarchy.
2. Keep the operator oriented with stable navigation and consistent labels.
3. Prefer dense, scannable information over decorative presentation.
4. Surface state, failures, and unavailable data explicitly.
5. Preserve safe, familiar terminal interactions.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard operation is fundamental. Important state must remain understandable without color, labels must fit practical terminal widths, and copy should avoid unnecessary abbreviations where space permits.
